CODEXGRAM BETA — VISUAL DESIGN SET
Source: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-codexgram-beta-design.md
Created with built-in image_gen; raster visual designs.

Product: public photo communities for signed-in beta testers.
Primary users: community members sharing and discussing photos; community creators moderating their own spaces; app admins reviewing reports.
Primary action: publish one photo to a joined community.
Activation: create/verify account, choose a username, join at least one community, reach Home.
Recurring journey: newest-first community feed → photo → like or flat comment → in-app activity → photo.
Authentication: email/password, Google, Facebook; email verification and password recovery; missing provider requirements before onboarding.
Permissions: request camera or photo-library access when used; denied-state alternatives are on board 14.
Roles: creators remove content and ban members; admins resolve reports, remove content and suspend accounts.
Free English-only beta. No subscriptions, ads, video, albums, filters, DMs, following, private communities, threaded replies, remote push or automatic offline queue.

Design assumptions: violet brand; warm ivory surfaces; aperture wordmark; rounded modern sans-serif typography; expressive color-led community identities; sample names, photographs and copy; standard report reasons; Light/Dark/device appearance selection. Sample creation and creator/admin screens are alternate role branches, not one chronological session. These assumptions are design choices, not additional approved product requirements.

Product limits remain authoritative in the source spec: 10 successful new posts/day; 20 post/replacement photo uploads/day; 5 avatar uploads/day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Captions max 2,000 characters. Photo replacement retains engagement and shows Edited. Retry preserves the local draft without duplicating the post. Sign-out clears the account's local draft. Account deletion disables access immediately, removes authored content and archives created communities.
